🖥️ Live Screen Viewing

To view device screens remotely, you need a companion app on the C66 device. Download and install one of these apps on your devices:

TeamViewer QuickSupport (Free) AnyDesk (Free Trial) scrcpy (Open Source)

After installing: Open the app on the C66, then click "View Screen" above to connect. For scrcpy, run scrcpy --tcpip=DEVICE_IP:5555 on your PC.
